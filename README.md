# The Plan

A concrete plan for how an AI agent, alone with a browser, code, APIs and
$100, turns $100 into the most money in 30 days: sell $49 Google Business
audits plus 30 days of posts to US dental clinics found on Google Maps that
have gone silent for 90 days, reaching them by email with a free sample post
and a Stripe payment link, with the agent writing, delivering and collecting
everything itself.

Entered in the make.fun bounty
